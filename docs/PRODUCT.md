## 2. Product Vision [CONFIRMED]

PACT is a **personal operating system** designed to turn intentions into consistent, measurable action. 

The core thesis of PACT is that individuals generally know what they *should* do; the primary barrier to personal growth is not a lack of knowledge, but a breakdown in **consistency, follow-through, accountability, and momentum**.

PACT provides a disciplined, structured framework to:
- Define long-term and short-term commitments.
- Plan daily, weekly, and goal-oriented time allocations.
- Organize tasks within structured project boundaries.
- Measure follow-through with authoritative progress metrics.
- Track personal finances in relation to lifestyle goals and habits.
- Connect external progress signals (e.g., GitHub, Codeforces, LeetCode).
- Enforce meaningful accountability when commitments are missed.

PACT is explicitly designed to transcend basic CRUD to-do applications, operating instead as a comprehensive **Personal Operating System (OS)**.

## 3. Product Philosophy & Aesthetic Tone [CONFIRMED]

PACT's aesthetic and psychological philosophy is grounded in calm, premium, and intentional design:

### Desired Attributes
- **Premium**: High-end craftsmanship, polished micro-interactions, dark aesthetic.
- **Calm**: Low visual noise, thoughtful spacing, deliberate color usage.
- **Intelligent**: Context-aware metrics, automated tracking, zero manual clutter.
- **Focused**: Clear priority views, distraction-free planning environments.
- **Intentional**: Every element serves a specific purpose in building discipline.
- **Private & Trustworthy**: Data ownership is strict, sensitive details protected.

### Explicitly Excluded Attributes
- ❌ Childish or overly cartoonish elements.
- ❌ Aggressive, noisy gamification (e.g., loud streak animations, badges, spammy popups).
- ❌ Cluttered UI, cheap SaaS aesthetics, or generic layout templates.
- ❌ Overly aggressive, toxic motivational messaging.
